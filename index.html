    A LITTLE CONTEXT

    A clearer way to plan.

    JU Transport is an independent student project by Md. Jakir Hossain, bringing seven Dhaka–Jahangirnagar University routes into one easy-to-use guide.

    Routes & departure times

    The mapped paths and reference times come from this project’s December 2025 version. Current service days, holidays and stop changes have not been verified. Paths are indicative and return roads may differ.

    Check the official timetable

    Live tracking

    Select “Share bus location” only while you are on the bus. Your current GPS position, route, bus label and accuracy become public until you stop sharing or the location expires. Keep this page open; locked phones may pause updates. Only the latest point is displayed, with no journey history. Community reports are unverified; authority badges require account approval. “Show my location” remains private to your browser and does not start sharing.

    Weather & saved routes

    Weather and air quality are model estimates from Open-Meteo and CAMS for the campus area. Saved routes and your theme choice stay in this browser. After your first visit, the route guide can also work offline; background maps and updated weather require a connection.

    Guide updated October 2026. This is a software update date, not confirmation of the transport schedule.
